How to get the location from Google Maps:

  1. Open the Google Maps app (or maps.google.com) on your phone
  2. Find the property's location, then press and hold (long-press) the exact spot until a red pin drops
  3. A card pops up at the bottom showing coordinates, e.g. 6.5244, 3.3792 — tap the coordinates to copy them
  4. Come back here and paste them into the box below, then tap "Use this"

Alternative: if you tap Share on the pin instead and copy the link, that also works here as long as it contains "@" followed by two numbers (e.g. "@6.5244,3.3792") — short links like maps.app.goo.gl/... won't work since those need Google's own servers to open.

Don't have Google Maps or find this confusing? No wahala — just scroll down and tap directly on the map below to drop a pin instead.
