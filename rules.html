📜 Platform Rules

Following these rules keeps everyone on VerifyStay safe and protected.

"If I meet you on the app, we deal on the app."

Every rule below exists to protect this one principle.

👑 Landlords

  1. Follow all VerifyStay platform rules at all times.
  2. Only list properties you legally own or manage — documents are reviewed manually before any listing goes live.
  3. Keep your listing information accurate; report changes (rent, availability) promptly.
  4. If you authorize an agent to list on your behalf, you may withdraw that authorization after a minimum of 6 months.
  5. All communication and rent agreements must happen through the app — this is what keeps you protected if a dispute arises.

🧑‍💼 Agents

  1. Follow both the landlord's instructions (for properties you list on their behalf) and VerifyStay platform rules.
  2. Never deal with a tenant or landlord outside the app once you've connected through VerifyStay — this is grounds for suspension.
  3. Represent properties honestly. A property that doesn't match its listed description can be reported and investigated.
  4. If listing for a landlord without their direct account, you must have their name, phone number, and their authorization on file.
  5. Repeated rule violations lead to probation, then possible account suspension for a period depending on severity.

🏠 Tenants

  1. Follow the agent's, landlord's, and VerifyStay's rules when engaging with a property.
  2. Report any property that doesn't match its description or address — this protects future tenants too.
  3. Report any agent or landlord who asks you to deal outside the app.
  4. Be respectful during negotiations and disputes — abusive language can lock your access to a chat thread.
  5. Keep proof of all agreements (screenshots, receipts) made through the app.

⚠️ What happens if a rule is broken?

  • Dealing outside the app: First offense = warning + temporary suspension. Repeat offenses = longer suspension, up to permanent removal.
  • False or mismatched listings: Property is flagged, hidden from search, and investigated before being reinstated or removed.
  • Abusive conduct in disputes: Chat access may be locked and the case escalated for manual review.
