📜 Platform Rules

Following these rules keeps everyone on VerifyStay safe and protected.

"If I meet you on the app, we deal on the app."

Every rule below exists to protect this one principle.

👑 For Landlords 🧑‍💼 For Agents 🏠 For Tenants 💰 Fees & Pricing 🔒 Payment & Escrow 🤝 Caretaker Role 📝 Digital Agreements 🚩 Flags, Bans & Trust 🏘️ Listings & Verification

👑 Landlords

  1. Follow all VerifyStay platform rules at all times.
  2. Only list properties you legally own or manage. Listings go live immediately, but every listing's documents are still checked afterward — if something doesn't match up, the listing comes down and an admin flag is added to your account.
  3. Keep your listing information accurate; update rent, availability, and unit counts promptly.
  4. You can list a property yourself, or hand day-to-day management to an agent through a signed Caretaker Agreement (see below) — either way, you stay the account owner.
  5. All communication and rent agreements must happen through the app — this is what keeps you protected if a dispute arises, and what makes escrow payment protection possible at all.

🧑‍💼 Agents

  1. Follow both the landlord's instructions (for properties you manage on their behalf) and VerifyStay's platform rules.
  2. Never deal with a tenant or landlord outside the app once you've connected through VerifyStay — this is grounds for an admin flag and possible suspension.
  3. You set your own commission percentage when listing or accepting a Caretaker Agreement. Staying at or under your state's commission cap unlocks the Caretaker Role — a recurring, protected income arrangement (see below). Charging above the cap means you can still list and manage properties, just without that protection.
  4. Represent properties honestly. A property that doesn't match its listed description can be reported and investigated.
  5. Repeated rule violations lead to admin flags, then suspension — see Flags & Bans below.

🏠 Tenants

  1. Follow the agent's, landlord's, and VerifyStay's rules when engaging with a property.
  2. You must be logged in to browse listings — this keeps the marketplace limited to verified, accountable accounts.
  3. Report any property that doesn't match its description or address, and report any agent or landlord who asks you to deal outside the app.
  4. Log repair requests through the app, not informally — this also affects your Service/Repair Fee rate at renewal (see Fees below).
  5. Keep proof of all agreements (they're saved in your My Agreements page automatically once signed).

🔒 Payment & Escrow

Not yet connected to a real payment provider — this section describes the intended flow, currently running in demo mode with no real money movement, ready to switch on once a licensed escrow provider is integrated.

  1. Once a tenancy agreement is signed by both sides, the tenant pays the full amount shown into escrow.
  2. Funds are held — for everyone, including VerifyStay's share — until the tenant confirms they've received keys and checked in.
  3. Tapping "I've Checked In" releases the held funds to the landlord, agent (if any), and VerifyStay.
  4. Tapping "Request Refund" at any point before check-in reverses the full amount back to the tenant. This option disappears once check-in is confirmed — anything wrong after that point goes through the normal dispute process instead.

🤝 Caretaker Role

A protected, recurring arrangement for agents managing a property long-term:

  1. Only available if the agent's commission is at or below the cap for that property's state.
  2. If a landlord ends the arrangement before a renewal, the agent is still owed a pro-rated share for the months they served — calculated as (months served ÷ 12) × commission % × the next actual rent payment, not an old or estimated rent.
  3. That pro-rated amount becomes due automatically the moment the property's next rent renewal is recorded — even if a different agent has since taken over.
  4. A property with an unpaid caretaker debt can't have the Caretaker Role reactivated until that debt is settled.

📝 Digital Agreements

  1. Caretaker handoffs (landlord → agent) and tenancy agreements (landlord/agent → tenant) are prepared in-app from a template, then sent for the other party to review.
  2. Accepting an agreement requires typing your full legal name as your e-signature — saved with a timestamp as part of the record.
  3. You can Accept, Decline, or ask to Renegotiate (which opens a direct chat to discuss terms before a new agreement is sent).
  4. All your agreements — sent and received — are always visible in My Agreements.

🚩 Flags, Bans & Trust

  • Red flags are user-submitted reports (against a listing or a person) — public, visible on anyone's profile, but never reveal who filed them.
  • Black flags (admin flags) are issued by VerifyStay after reviewing a confirmed problem — private, visible only to the account owner.
  • 3 black flags = a suspension: 2 weeks the first time, 4 weeks the second, 6 the third, and so on.
  • Every profile — tenant, agent, or landlord — is publicly viewable, so anyone can check ratings, red flags, and verification status before dealing with someone.
  • Landlords/agents can rate tenants after renting to them, the same way tenants rate properties.

🏘️ Listings & Verification

  • Listings go live immediately — no waiting for approval.
  • Every new (or edited) listing shows a "Not yet verified" badge for the first 4 hours, then it disappears automatically. Documents are still checked behind the scenes during that window and after.
  • Required at posting: property photo, building name, and a utility bill as proof of address.
  • If a listing is later found to be false or misleading, it comes down and the account gets an admin (black) flag.
